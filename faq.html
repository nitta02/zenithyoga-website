Getting Started

Can I join as a beginner?

Absolutely. Our beginner-friendly classes introduce poses, breathing, and alignment at a comfortable pace. Your instructor will offer modifications throughout the session.

How early should I arrive?

Please arrive 10–15 minutes before your class. This gives you time to check in, settle into the studio, and speak with your instructor about any needs.

Classes

What should I bring to class?

Wear comfortable clothing and bring a water bottle. Mats and essential props are available at the studio, though you’re welcome to bring your own.

Do you offer online classes?

Yes. Private sessions and selected group programs can be joined online, making it easy to practice with Zenith Wellness wherever you are.
