Privacy Policy

At Zenith Wellness, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our website and in using our services. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account, book a session, or communicate with us. This may include:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number).
  • Billing and payment information (processed securely via our payment partners).
  • Health and wellness goals you choose to share with your practitioners.
  • Technical data automatically collected when you interact with our digital platforms (e.g., IP address, browser type).

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used to deliver, maintain, and improve our wellness services. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Facilitate class bookings, workshop registrations, and retreat reservations.
  • Communicate important updates regarding your schedule or account status.
  • Personalize your wellness journey based on your stated preferences.
  • Process transactions securely and prevent fraudulent activity.
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve our digital interfaces and physical studio experiences.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data in the following limited circumstances:

  • With service providers who assist us in operating our business (e.g., scheduling software, payment processors).
  • When required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Zenith Wellness, our clients, or others.
  • With your explicit consent for specific wellness programs or partnerships.

4. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to access the personal information we hold about you.
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate data.
  • The right to request deletion of your personal data, subject to certain legal exceptions.
  • The right to opt-out of marketing communications at any time.
