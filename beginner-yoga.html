About the Class

This class is designed to demystify yoga and build a strong, safe foundation for your practice. We focus on breaking down basic postures, understanding healthy alignment for your unique body, and linking movement with breath.

Expect a gentle, supportive environment where questions are encouraged. Our instructional style prioritizes accessibility, ensuring you feel confident and comfortable as you explore what yoga can offer you.

What You’ll Learn

  • Basic yoga poses
  • Breathing techniques
  • Body alignment
  • Relaxation
  • Stretching basics
