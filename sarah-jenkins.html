About Sarah Jenkins

Lead Instructor

Sarah Jenkins

Lead Yoga Therapist & Studio Founder

Biography

Sarah's journey into wellness began over two decades ago when she sought refuge from a high-stress corporate career. Discovering the profound impact of mindful movement, she dedicated her life to understanding the intricacies of the human body and spirit. Her teaching style is deeply rooted in compassion, encouraging students to listen to their own bodies while gently pushing their boundaries. She believes that yoga is not just a physical practice, but a holistic approach to living a balanced and meaningful life.
